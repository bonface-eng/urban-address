# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Unified Business OS

## 1. Business Objectives
To provide a centralized, fraud-resistant management system for a multi-service facility encompassing a Lounge, Accessories Store, and Carwash.

## 2. User Types
- **Cashier**: POS operations for Store and Lounge.
- **Storekeeper**: Inventory management and stock audits.
- **Carwash Attendant**: Service tracking and job completion.
- **Supervisor**: Approval of refunds, voids, and petty cash.
- **Manager/Admin**: Full system access, employee management, and financial reporting.

## 3. Functional Requirements
### POS (Lounge & Store)
- Barcode scanning for store items.
- Table management for the lounge.
- Split billing and multi-method payments (Cash, Card, M-Pesa/Paystack).
### Carwash
- Vehicle registration and service selection.
- Attendant assignment and job status tracking.
### Finance & Fraud
- Petty cash request and approval workflow.
- Real-time fraud detection alerts for voids and high-value refunds.
- Shift reconciliation and cash variance reporting.

## 4. Hardware Requirements
- 80mm Thermal Receipt Printers (ESC/POS).
- 1D/2D USB/Bluetooth Barcode Scanners.
- Electronic Cash Drawers (RJ11).
